## Problem Title
SIH 26033: Multiple intermediaries reduce farmers' earnings and increase consumer prices.

## Problem Creator's Organization
Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food & Public Distribution

## Theme
Agriculture, FoodTech & Rural Development

## Problem Description
Expected Solution: Create a digital marketplace that:

- Connects farmers/FPOs directly with consumers and bulk buyers.
- Provides logistics support.
- Uses AI for demand forecasting and route optimization.

Benefits:

- Better prices for farmers.
- Lower prices for consumers.
- Reduced supply chain inefficiencies.

## Proposed Solution
**KisanSetu – "A Bridge from Farm to Fork"**
1. **Detailed explanation of the proposed solution:** A voice-first, multilingual marketplace where farmers/FPOs list produce by app, WhatsApp or IVR, buyers order directly, hubs grade and ship, and UPI escrow pays the farmer on delivery.
2. **How it addresses the problem:** Direct trade removes 3–4 intermediaries, AI forecasting reduces wastage, and pooled logistics cuts costs for farmers and consumers.
3. **Innovation and uniqueness of the solution:**
   - Fair Price Meter: shows where every rupee goes.
   - Pre-Harvest Booking: guaranteed demand before harvest.
   - Spoilage-Aware Pricing and Group Buying: less waste, better bulk prices.
   - QR Traceability and Voice-First Access: trust and inclusion.

## Technical Approach
![kisansetu-ecosystem](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f5d7a6e3-44e5-413b-aacd-07dec1986ecd)

1. **Technologies to be used:** React Native, Node.js, PostgreSQL/MongoDB, Python (Prophet, OR-Tools, CNN grading), Bhashini, WhatsApp/IVR, Razorpay UPI, AWS/Docker.
2. **Methodology and process for implementation** **(Flow Charts/Images/ working prototype)**: Onboard → List and AI grade → Match buyers → Order and escrow → Pooled delivery → QR payout → Feedback to forecasting.

## Feasibility and Viability
1. **Analysis of the feasibility of the idea:** High smartphone/UPI reach, FPOs and e-NAM as aggregation points, and mature, affordable technologies.
2. **Potential challenges and risks:** Low digital literacy, poor connectivity, cold-chain gaps, resistance from existing trade networks.
3. **Strategies for overcoming these challenges:** Voice-first regional UI with offline mode, "Kisan Mitra" village agents, local transport partners, escrow and ratings, district-wise pilot.

## Impact and Benefits
1. **Potential impact on the target audience:** Estimated 20–30% higher farmer income, fresher and cheaper produce for consumers, reliable and traceable supply for bulk buyers.
2. **Benefits of the solution (social, economic, environmental, etc.):** Social inclusion of small farmers, fewer middlemen and new rural jobs, less food wastage and lower fuel use.
